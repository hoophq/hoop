  curl -s http://127.0.0.1:8009{{ include "hoopcontrolplane.basePath" . }}/api/publicserverinfo | grep application_mode

This deployment carries no traffic: no gRPC on :8010, no protocol proxies, and
of the six transport plugins only Slack is started. The routes that need the
transport answer with an error rather than hanging; see ADR-0024.
